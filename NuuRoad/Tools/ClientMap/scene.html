Fly like a character with no gravity: drag to look round, W/S forward and back where you look, A/D step sideways, Q/E turn, Space/R up, X/F down, Shift faster; the wheel sets the speed.
Places: red lines, red junctions, blue bends, yellow ends, green ways in, purple lifts. Caves are found and marked in the zone browser.
Models baked with wow.export's exporters (MIT); drawn with three.js (MIT).
